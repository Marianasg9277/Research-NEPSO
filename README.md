# 📊 NEPSO Research Projects (2022 - 2025)

### 📌 About the Initiative
**NEPSO** (Nossa Escola Pesquisa a Sua Opinião) is a project focused on developing research methodologies, public opinion polling, and data analysis. Through this initiative, I conducted comprehensive studies on relevant social themes, applying critical thinking, teamwork, and data collection techniques.

---

### 🏆 Projects & Achievements

#### 1. Music Therapy Research (2023 - 2024)
* **Supervisor:** Prof.ª Lídia Santos
* **Team:** 4M's (Simão Ribeiro, Afonso Gouveia, Mariana Gomes, Ariana Vieira)
* **Goal:** Investigated the impact and public perception of music therapy.
* **Skills Developed:** Teamwork, problem-solving, and social awareness.
* **Award:** 🥇 **1st Place National Award** (Prize: Trip to Brazil + €500).

#### 2. Job Market for People with Disabilities (2022 - 2023)
* **Supervisor:** Prof.º Gil Santos
* **Team:** Leões sem Juba (Simão Ribeiro, Afonso Gouveia, Mariana Gomes, Ariana Vieira)
* **Goal:** Evaluated the knowledge and opinion regarding the inclusion of people with disabilities in the workforce.
* **Skills Developed:** Research methodology, data collection and analysis, critical thinking, organization, and time management.
* **Award:** 🥉 **3rd Place National Award** (Prize: €200).
